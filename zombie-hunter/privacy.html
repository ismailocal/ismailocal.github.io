Zombie Hunter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Zombie Hunter (Android package com.ismail.zombiavcisi), a free mobile game developed by an independent developer (ismailocal; "we", "the developer").

Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no online profile.
  • Your progress, settings and high scores are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to deliver and measure those ads.
  • An optional one-time purchase that removes interstitial ads is handled by Google Play.

Information the game itself stores

The game saves your settings, records and unfinished run in a file on your device. This file never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers for this game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show rewarded and interstitial ads. To do this, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived,
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version),
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (which ads were shown or tapped, crashes and performance of the ad SDK).

Google uses this data to serve ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the game shows a Google consent message. You can accept or refuse personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the game's main menu.
  • All users: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device settings under Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

In-app purchase

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase belongs to your Google account, so it can be restored on another device.

Children

Zombie Hunter is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you. Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account and device settings described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
