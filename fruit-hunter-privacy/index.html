Fruit Hunter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Fruit Hunter (Android package co.median.android.zpppwqq), a free mobile game developed by an independent developer (ismailocal; "we", "the developer").

Summary

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your settings, best score and fruit count are stored on your device.
  • If you choose a nickname, that nickname and your best score are shown on a public online leaderboard.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to deliver and measure those ads.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your settings (sound, vibration), best score, total fruit collected, highest level and your nickname in a file on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Online leaderboard (Google Firebase)

The global leaderboard is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (part of Google Firebase). It holds only the nickname you choose and your best score, with the time it was last updated. A nickname is optional; without one, nothing is written to the leaderboard. Everyone who opens the leaderboard can see the top nicknames and scores, so please do not use your real name or other personal information as a nickname.

When the game reads or writes the leaderboard, Google receives your IP address as part of the network request. Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy information.

To have your nickname and score removed from the leaderboard, e-mail us the nickname at the address below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show a banner ad, interstitial ads between runs and an optional rewarded ad that gives you a second chance. To do this, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived,
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version),
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (which ads were shown or tapped, crashes and performance of the ad SDK).

Google uses this data to serve ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the game shows a Google consent message. You can accept or refuse personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the game's main menu.
  • All users: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device settings under Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Children

Fruit Hunter is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a nickname, contact us and we will remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept until you ask us to remove them. Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account and device settings described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
